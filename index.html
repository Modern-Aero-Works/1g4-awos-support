About the App

1G4 AWOS provides convenient access to aviation weather observations for Grand Canyon West Airport (1G4/K1G4), Arizona.

The app displays available weather data from the airport's AWOSNET system, including altimeter, wind, visibility, sky conditions, temperature, dew point, and density altitude.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't the weather updating?

Weather updates depend on the availability of the AWOSNET data source and your internet connection. Open the app and pull down to request a new observation.

Why does my widget show older information?

iOS controls when widgets refresh. Open the main app to retrieve newer information. Always check the observation timestamp before relying on displayed data.

Does the app work offline?

The app requires an internet connection to retrieve new observations. Previously saved information may remain visible.

Weather Data Source

Weather information is obtained from the Grand Canyon West Airport AWOSNET service.
